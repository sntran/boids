Rock Patrol

A ship in a field of rocks, and a queen with drones that hunt it. Each thing is one Erlang process that senses, decides and acts.

Or use the arrow keys and the space bar. With no key for 5 seconds, the autopilot flies the ship.

The ship is a boid that you control

Its module has the behavior gen_boid. Its force is a rule of its own: a function that reads your keys. The autopilot is a list of rules of the library.

A shot sends a message

A shot senses the rock that it touches, and sends hit to that process. The rock starts two smaller rocks, and stops. The flock removes a boid that stops.

The drones have a relation

Each drone has the relation guards to its queen, and follows it. A drone that sees the ship changes its rules, and hunts. The lines show the relations.

The flock is a graph

The number of swarms comes from boids:graph/2 and graph:components/1, one time each second.
