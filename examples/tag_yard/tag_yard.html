Tag Yard

Play tag in a school yard. You are the gold child. Run from the child that is "it". When "it" touches you, you are it: then you must touch another child. A child that was it a moment ago cannot be tagged back.

Mode

One child is it. Be free when the bell rings after 90 seconds.

Run from the child that is it.

Stamina

Use the arrow keys or W, A, S and D to move. Hold Shift to run: running uses stamina, and the stamina comes back when you walk or stand.

A state that goes by a message

Each child is a gen_boid process with its role as its state: runner, it, or frozen. When "it" touches a runner, it casts {tagged, Name} to the process of that runner. The runner becomes it, and the old "it" becomes a runner. No process decides who is it.

Rules that change with the state

Each role has its own rules. A runner flees "it" with the rule evade, "it" chases the nearest runner with pursue, and a frozen child has no rules. When the role changes, the child changes its rules.

What the neighbors see

The data of a child shows its role, if it cannot be tagged now ("no tag back"), and if it is a tired "it". A child that is it for a long time gets slower, and the runners come nearer to it. Then it can tag a runner, and the game does not stop.

A role that moves through the crowd

The role "it" goes from child to child, and the crowd moves away from it as a wave. You are a child too: your force is a rule of its own, a function that reads your keys. The director counts the time and the score, and makes a child it when no child is it.
