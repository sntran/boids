Hive Guard

Waves of beetles walk along the path to your hive, on the right side. Select a bee, then click the field to place it. A bee guards its post, chases the beetles that it sees, and stings them. A killed beetle gives nectar.

Connecting

The hive waits for the first wave.

Click the field to place a scout. You can place 12 bees.

The beetles follow a path

Each beetle has the rule follow with the points of the path, and stays with its swarm with separation, alignment and a little cohesion. A large beetle is slower, and the number on it is its health.

A bee pursues a group

A bee at its post has the rule arrive to its post. When it sees a beetle, it changes its rules to pursue with the target {group, beetles}: the nearest beetle that it sees.

A sting is a message

A bee that touches a beetle finds its process, and sends it a sting. The beetle loses health, and stops at 0. Its process tells the game, and the game gives you nectar.

You start boids while the world runs

Each bee that you place is a new gen_boid process in the world. The waves are new processes too: the game starts each beetle at the start of the path.
