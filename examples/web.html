Boids in Flight

Each boid is one Erlang process. It senses its neighbors, decides, and acts, on its own clock.

The birds follow rules

Each bird has five rules: avoid, evade, separation, alignment and cohesion. No process tells the flock where to go.

The hawks decide

A hawk is a module with the behavior gen_boid. It hunts while it has energy, then it rests. The bar shows its energy.

The convoy has relations

Each boid of the convoy has the relation follows to the boid before it. The lines show these relations.

The flock is a graph

The number of groups comes from boids:graph/2 and graph:components/1, one time each second.
