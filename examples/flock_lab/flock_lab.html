        For grown-ups
        
      
        Each sheep, wolf and dog is an Erlang process with the behavior gen_boid. A block is a rule of the library, and its strength is the weight of the rule.
        
    
        
    
        
      
        The ideas of the levels
        
      
        A program as a list of steps, numbers that change a behavior, many rules at one time, a result that emerges, order and priority, conditions, relations, and a program for another animal.
        
    
        
    
        
      
        How it works
        
      
        "When I see" changes the rules of a sheep in handle_decide/2. "Follow the leader" uses a relation. "Walk behind the sheep" is a rule that is a function.
