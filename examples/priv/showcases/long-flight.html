Long Flight

Lead a flock to the exit of each land, on the right side. You do not control a bird: you give the flock its rules. After each land, take one card and put the rules in an order.

The rules of the flock

The first rule uses the force first.

    The rules are data

    Your deck is the list of the rules of each bird. A card adds a rule, or changes a weight. The library checks each rule.

    The order is the priority

    The birds have combine => priority: each rule in the order of the list takes a part of the force, and the last rules get what stays.

    A boid changes while it runs

    With the card "Scatter", a bird that sees a hawk has other rules first. With "Elders", each bird has the relation follows to an elder.

    Boids send messages, and stop

    A hawk sends caught to the process of the bird that it touches. A bird that is safe, caught or in a rock stops, and the world removes it.
